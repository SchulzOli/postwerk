features:
  - title: Campaigns across channels
    details: One goal, one message and one budget for social posts, Google and Meta ads, YouTube, print, letters and spots, with approvals and results per channel.
  - title: One brand character
    details: A brand kit with tone of voice, colors, logo and a recurring character that appears in every spot, ad and print piece.
  - title: Print and post
    details: Print-ready PDFs with bleed and CMYK colors, and personal letters sent by post through Pingen.
  - title: AI studio, if you want it
    details: Claude drafts ads, print texts, spot scripts and post ideas in your brand's voice. Optional; every draft needs your approval.
  - title: One canvas
    details: Networks, accounts, flows, the composer, the calendar and your posts live in one zoomable world.
  - title: 15 networks
    details: Mastodon, Bluesky, Telegram and Discord right away; Facebook, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Google Business Profile, Pinterest and Reddit with your own apps or through the Zernio bridge.
  - title: Your apps, your choice
    details: Each server registers its own developer apps. For your own accounts many networks need no review; the guides show the shortest way for each.
  - title: Ready for reviews
    details: Every server gets an about page, privacy policy, terms, imprint and data deletion page, filled in from its own settings.
  - title: Flows and calendar
    details: Reusable publishing pipelines, per-network versions of a post, and a calendar to drag posts around.
  - title: Open source
    details: MIT licensed, in English and German, with themes as plugins.
