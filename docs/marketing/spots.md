# Video and radio spots

Spots belong to a campaign, like [print pieces](print.md). MarketingAsAService does not produce the video or the audio itself. It helps you write a script that fits the format and the brand, hands it to whoever produces it, and keeps the finished file with the campaign. A video spot can then go to YouTube for video ads.

## Formats

| Format | Length | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Video spot 6 s, 15 s, 30 s | 6, 15 or 30 seconds | YouTube video ads (6 s fits bumper ads) |
| TV spot | 30 seconds | TV |
| Cinema spot | 30 seconds | Cinema |
| Radio spot 20 s, 30 s | 20 or 30 seconds | Radio |

## Writing the script

A script is a list of scenes. Each scene has its length in seconds, what we see (video only), what is said, text on screen (video only), music and sound, and whether the brand character appears.

While you write, the editor checks:

- **Length.** The scenes add up to exactly the spot's length.
- **Pace.** A voice-over fits its scene at about 2.5 words a second, a calm pace for ads. The counter next to each voice-over shows words used and words that fit.
- **The brand character.** It appears in at least one scene, and its catchphrase from the brand kit is said or shown. The same character in every spot is what makes people recognize the brand.
- **The produced file**, once it exists.

Directions for production (music, voice, pace, look) go below the scenes.

## Briefing for production

**Download briefing** creates a PDF for a studio, an agency or a speaker:

- campaign, format, length, key message and audience
- the brand: tagline, tone of voice, do's and don'ts, and the colors with their hex codes (video only)
- the brand character: what it is, look, personality, voice, catchphrase, what it never does, and up to two reference pictures from the brand kit
- the script as a table with time codes
- the directions

## The produced spot

Upload the finished spot to the piece: a video (MP4, MOV or WebM) or, for radio, audio (MP3, M4A or WAV, up to 100 MB). Saving sends the spot back to *draft*, so an owner or admin approves the final version.

Audio files belong to radio spots only; they cannot be attached to social media posts.

## YouTube

Video ads on YouTube play a video from a YouTube channel. Once a video spot is approved and its file attached:

- **Upload to YouTube** posts it to a connected YouTube channel, unlisted by default, as a post of the campaign. The page shows the upload's progress and then the video's link.
- Or paste the link of a video that is already on YouTube.

Uploading needs a connected YouTube channel; see [YouTube](../networks/google.md#youtube).
