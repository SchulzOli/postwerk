# Code of conduct

Postwerk follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the people who use Postwerk. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, [@SchulzOli](https://github.com/SchulzOli), privately on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block people who break these rules.
